PT CPI Sales Target & Gap Analytics Portal

A cloud-based sales target management and analytics platform built with Google Apps Script and Google Sheets. The system enables hierarchical target submission, sales performance tracking, gap analysis, KPI monitoring, and executive-level reporting across TSM, RSM, and Head Office teams.

Md. Masum Billah · June 6, 2026 · 3 min read

Building a centralized sales target management and performance analytics platform using Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, and a custom business intelligence dashboard.

Executive Summary

PT Consumer Products Industries required a centralized system for collecting monthly sales targets, monitoring field performance, tracking reporting compliance, and analyzing sales gaps across multiple regions.

To solve this challenge, I designed and developed a cloud-based Sales Target & Gap Analytics Portal that enables Territory Sales Managers (TSMs) to submit targets while providing executives with real-time visibility into organizational performance.

1. Business Challenge

Managing sales targets across multiple territories and field teams was becoming increasingly difficult through spreadsheets and manual reporting processes.

Management lacked visibility into target achievement, sales performance, and submission compliance at both regional and national levels.

  • Manual target collection process
  • No centralized reporting system
  • Difficulty tracking TSM submissions
  • Limited performance visibility
  • No real-time target vs actual comparison
  • Time-consuming report consolidation

As the organization grew, spreadsheet-driven processes could no longer support business decision-making effectively.

2. Solution Architecture

I developed a centralized web application using Google Workspace technologies and a custom analytics engine.

  • Google Apps Script Backend
  • Google Sheets Database Layer
  • HTML & JavaScript Frontend
  • Tailwind CSS User Interface
  • Role-Based Authentication System
  • Dynamic Analytics Engine

The platform serves as a single source of truth for sales targets, reporting compliance, and performance analytics.

3. Multi-Level User Management

The application was designed with hierarchical access control to ensure each user only sees information relevant to their role.

  • Territory Sales Manager (TSM)
  • Regional Sales Manager (RSM)
  • Office Users
  • System Administrators

User authentication dynamically determines which dashboard and features are accessible after login.

4. Dynamic Target Entry Portal

One of the core challenges was simplifying target submission for field teams.

Instead of entering data into multiple spreadsheets, TSMs interact with a dynamic matrix interface that automatically generates target entry grids based on assigned dealers, sales representatives, territories, and products.

  • Auto-generated target matrix
  • Dealer-wise planning
  • Product-wise allocation
  • Territory-level targeting
  • Historical submission loading

5. Executive Analytics Dashboard

For management, I developed an executive dashboard focused on performance monitoring and decision support.

  • Target Volume KPI
  • Financial Target Value
  • Actual Sales Performance
  • Gap Analysis
  • Achievement Percentage
  • Submission Tracking

Executives can instantly identify underperforming territories and take corrective action without waiting for manual reports.

6. Gap Analysis Engine

The most valuable feature of the platform is the Gap Analytics Engine.

By combining target data with actual sales records, the system automatically calculates performance variance across every level of the organization.

  • Target Quantity
  • Target Value
  • Actual Quantity
  • Actual Revenue
  • Sales Gap
  • Achievement Percentage

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable business intelligence.

7. Reporting Compliance Tracker

A dedicated submission tracker monitors reporting compliance across all active TSMs.

Management can instantly identify which territories have submitted monthly targets and which teams require follow-up.

  • Submission Monitoring
  • Pending TSM Detection
  • Compliance Tracking
  • Real-Time Status Updates

8. Business Impact

The implementation significantly improved operational efficiency and reporting visibility throughout the organization.

  • Reduced manual reporting effort
  • Improved reporting compliance
  • Faster executive decision-making
  • Real-time performance visibility
  • Centralized business intelligence
  • Scalable reporting architecture

9. Conclusion

The PT CPI Sales Target & Gap Analytics Portal transformed a fragmented reporting process into a centralized analytics platform.

By combining automation, business intelligence, and cloud-based technologies, the system now provides both operational teams and executives with the tools needed to plan, monitor, and optimize sales performance effectively.

The real value of analytics is not measuring performance — it is enabling better business decisions.
