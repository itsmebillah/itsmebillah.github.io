What is Business Inteligence?

Business Intelligence enables organizations to move from data overload to actionable insight, supporting smarter strategies and faster decisions.

Md. Masum Billah · December 23, 2026 · 2 min read

Category: Business Analytics

Before diving deeper into what Business Intelligence is, let us first look at some powerful statistics that highlight why BI has become essential for modern organizations.

  • The global BI market was expected to reach USD 29.48 billion.
  • Over 46% of businesses already use BI tools as a core strategy.
  • Nearly 90% of sales and marketing teams rely on BI for effectiveness.
  • More than 2.7 million jobs now require data analytics skills.
  • Average ROI from BI and analytics can reach up to 1300%.

These numbers clearly show that Business Intelligence is no longer optional. It is now a strategic necessity across industries.

When Big Data is integrated with BI, organizations can improve decision-making, uncover new insights, and significantly enhance productivity.

Business Intelligence: Definition

“Business Intelligence is not just about turning data into information. Organizations need data to impact how their business operates and responds to the changing marketplace.”
— Gerald Cohen

Business Intelligence is not merely about converting data into reports. It is about transforming data into actionable insights that support strategic and tactical decisions.

BI tools analyze datasets and present insights through dashboards, reports, charts, and visualizations, giving leaders a clear view of organizational performance.

Companies that rely on inaccurate or incomplete information are far more likely to make poor decisions compared to organizations that leverage BI effectively.

BI: A Rapidly Growing Field

For Business Intelligence to be effective, data must be accurate, timely, and well-structured. However, real-world data is often messy and unstructured.

BI platforms help organizations overcome this challenge and avoid the classic problem of “Garbage In, Garbage Out.”

BI Software and Systems

Business Intelligence consists of several key software components:

  • Dashboards and scorecards
  • Data visualization tools
  • Reporting systems
  • Data mining solutions
  • ETL (Extract-Transform-Load) tools
  • OLAP (Online Analytical Processing)

What is Power BI?

“Enable everyone at every level of your organization to make confident decisions using up-to-the-minute analysis.”

Power BI is a Business Intelligence and data visualization tool developed by Microsoft. It converts data from multiple sources into interactive dashboards and analytical reports.

Power BI is available in Desktop, Cloud (SaaS), and Mobile versions, making it highly accessible.

Why Power BI?

  • Real-time dashboard updates
  • Built-in SaaS dashboards
  • Strong security and compliance support
  • Natural language data queries
  • Secure cloud and on-premise data connections

Conclusion

Business Intelligence, particularly tools like Power BI, continues to be recognized as a market leader. Learning BI opens doors to high-impact analytics roles and long-term career growth.

With the right tools, skills, and mindset, BI empowers organizations to move from data overload to clarity and confident decisions.
